MANUAL STAIR ROUTES
-------------------

- Descent -> Lobby: x=380/y=1270, x=530/y=1120, x=680/y=980.
- Lobby -> Ascent: x=1000/y=735, x=850/y=570, x=700/y=405.

The center shaft stays open. Steps are one-way platforms, not wall floors.
Lift remains the primary fast/reliable hub traversal choice.

ONE-WAY PLATFORM SYSTEM
-----------------------
New platform types:
- solid: full normal collision from top, underside, and sides.
- oneWay: collision only when player is falling onto its top surface.

One-way behavior:
- Player jumps upward through one-way lift pads and stepping stones.
- Player lands on the top while falling.
- Player can drop down with S + Jump or Down Arrow + Jump.
- Drop action starts a short 0.22-second collision-ignore timer for one-way
  platforms only. Solid wall floors remain solid.

INPUTS
------
A / D or Left / Right Arrow: Move.
W / Up Arrow / Space: Jump.
S / Down Arrow + Jump: Drop through one-way platform.
E: Interact.
R: Reset current scene.
Esc: Close overlay.
